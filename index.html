How it works

Cross it. Heat it. Hold it.

Cross it, it's yours

Start a workout on your iPhone or Apple Watch. Every unclaimed tile you cross is yours the moment you step on it. A tile is about a city block.

Come back, it heats up

Every visit adds power, and so does time spent working out on it. Your tiles glow like metal in a forge, until the ones you live on go white hot.

Spend your effort

Working out fills your energy bar, by heart rate if you wear a watch. Press and hold a tile to spend it: heat your own ground from anywhere, take an empty tile next to yours, or cool a rival's.

Fight for the edges

Work out on a rival's tile and it cools. At zero it's up for grabs, so walk it and it's yours. They can do the same to you, so keep your ground hot.

What makes it different

A few things I won't budge on.

You can't fake the work

Your watch reads your heart rate, and the game pays for effort, not speed. Get in a car and it notices: earning stops, and whatever you took from the passenger seat goes back.

Every kind of workout counts

A walk counts, and so does a run or a ride. The same effort for your body earns the same energy, whatever you're doing. No watch? Your phone still counts your steps and your route, at a lower rate.

No pay-to-win

Money will never buy heat or ground. People who actually exercise will always beat people who only pay.

You fight tiles, not people

There's no chat, and nothing in the game targets a person. When you lose ground, the game tells you how much, not who took it.

Why it exists

Built to make you healthier.

The heat is there to make coming back tomorrow feel like something. The real job is simpler: get you moving more, and still moving next month. When a feature would juice my numbers without making you any healthier, I don't ship it.

FAQ

So you have questions.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. Your iPhone counts your steps and your route on its own. A watch adds your heart rate, which earns energy at the full rate, and it can run the whole workout while your phone stays home.

Does my heat fade if I take a week off?

No. Your tiles keep their heat. You only lose a tile when a rival cools it to zero and takes it.

Why doesn't the game have sound?

Because you're probably listening to music during your workout.

What will it cost?

It's free in the beta. Later there'll be an optional subscription to support me (hi, I'm Ben!). It will never sell anyone an advantage over someone who works out.

Where does my data go?

Your health data never leaves your phone. The game service knows which tiles you hold and, for about a day, which ones you crossed. It never stores your raw location. If you share a workout, that workout's stats and a simplified route go with the link, which expires after 14 days. I will never sell your data to anyone, nor sell the company to anyone who would.
